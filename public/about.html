Our Story

Founded in 2012, Oaklands Nursery and Primary School began as a shared vision between two remarkable women: Hajjat Janat Ssali and Hajjat Rehema Ssebagala. Through their unwavering dedication, hard work, and personal sacrifice, they transformed a simple idea into a thriving educational reality. Today, their legacy continues to nurture young minds and build a foundation for life.
